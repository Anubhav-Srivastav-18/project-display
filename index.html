Questions

Can people see my number?

Yes. Anyone who scans can read it, because it is stored in the QR. Hiding it fully needs a call-forwarding service such as Twilio plus a backend.

Do I need a website?

Not for WhatsApp or Call stickers. Only the details page needs hosting.

Does the QR expire?

No. It has no server behind it, so it works as long as the number stays the same.
